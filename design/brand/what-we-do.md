# What We Do

**Core message:** We tell you whether your space can make money from EV charging — then match you with the operator best suited to build and run it.

---

## In one line
An independent EV charging advisory and matchmaking layer — we assess sites, pick the right Charge Point Operator (CPO) for each one, and connect the two.

## What we are not
- Not a CPO. We don't own the network.
- Not tied to one brand of charger.
- Not a hardware reseller pushing stock.

## What we actually do

**1. Site assessment**
- Evaluate whether a location is viable for EV charging
- Assess power availability, traffic and dwell time, parking layout, local EV density
- Give a straight answer — including "not yet" or "not here"
- Produce a site report detailed enough to be used for loan sanctioning

**2. CPO matching**
- Determine which operator from our partner network suits that specific site
- Match on hardware type (AC vs DC), commercial model, coverage, and service capability
- Recommend one, with the reasoning shown

**3. Connection and handover**
- Introduce the site owner to the chosen CPO
- The CPO delivers: charger supply, installation, CMS, ongoing service
- We stay involved through commissioning

## Who we serve
- **Property owners / RWAs** — apartment complexes, malls, offices, hotels with parking to monetise
- **Fleets & businesses** — depots and commercial fleets electrifying operations
- **CPOs** — pre-qualified sites matched to their hardware and economics
- **EV owners / homeowners** — home charging, installed correctly
- **Banks** — site reports rigorous enough to underwrite a loan against

## The problem we solve
**Uncertain ROI.** Site owners don't know whether charging will pay back, and can't tell one operator's pitch from another's. We remove the guesswork before any money is spent.
