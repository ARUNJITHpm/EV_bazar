02 — Problem

₹20–40 lakh committed on a vendor’s spreadsheet.

A land owner on NH-66 near Technopark is pitched a 60 kW DC charger. The vendor’s sheet uses a P50 utilisation, ignores that sanctioned load is billed in kVA, and skips the 8 chargers already within 3 km — the nearest 246 m away. The demand charge alone is ₹2.4–4 lakh a year before a single unit is sold.

  • 01Nobody tells them the number the site has to beat.
  • 02Everyone selling to them earns on a yes.
  • 03Lenders have no underwriting standard for a charger at all.

One painful, specific scenario. This is our real demo site — Kazhakkoottam, Thiruvananthapuram.

03 — Solution

A verdict that is allowed to say no.

Breakeven is pure arithmetic over the state’s real tariff order. A model predicts one thing only — kWh per connector per day, as a P10–P90 band. The verdict is decided by the pessimistic P10, never the median.

07 — Business model

Operators pay for the bill audit now; expansion teams and lenders subscribe to screening — and a “don’t build” earns the same as a yes.

Rung 1 · cash now
Tariff Audit

Existing operators. Wrong tariff category, oversized sanctioned load, missed ToD. “We found ₹2.8 lakh a year in your bill.” Needs no model.

Rung 2 · the business
Portfolio screening

CPO expansion teams, banks & NBFCs, OMC dealer programmes, fleets. “Rank these 200 sites” — subscription or API.

Rung 3 · upside
Operator-matching fees

Paid by operators per matched site — priced separately from the report, with the verdict distribution published.

How you make money, in one sentence. The free pin-drop and retail report are lead capture, not the business.
