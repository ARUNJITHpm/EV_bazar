What the live review found

  1. The wrong introduction. The first screen shows a sample location, model metadata and a large DON'T BUILD verdict rather than the customer's question.
  2. Too much reading. Detailed evidence, assumptions and repeated explanations dominate the first visit.
  3. Mobile comparisons are difficult. Some important table columns require sideways scrolling. Diagram labels shrink to roughly 6-7px high.
  4. The colors fit. Warm paper and deep slate communicate a serious, auditable service. Copper can give the primary homepage action more emphasis.

Clarity improvements implemented

  • The homepage now starts with the customer's question and one location action.
  • Three short benefits explain site suitability, costs and possible returns as scenarios, and operator comparison.
  • A clearly labelled sample preview appears below the customer introduction.
  • Report diagrams keep readable labels on mobile; wide diagrams and tables show a scroll hint when needed.
  • Detailed checklists remain available in expandable questions; coverage limits and commercial disclosures remain visible.

The homepage has 321 visible words with its detailed questions closed. The report keeps its full evidence and financial scenarios.
