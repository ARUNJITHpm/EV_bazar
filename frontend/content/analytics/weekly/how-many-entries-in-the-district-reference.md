This chart counts entries in our verified archived district reference, using the source linked beneath it. It is not a count of today's districts.

Local Government Directory (LGD) codes identify the entries. Names and boundaries may have changed since this snapshot. Comparing periods needs a dated mapping for renamed or split districts.

A reference helps connect records; it does not measure EV registrations, chargers or electricity use. Browse the [source register](/data/sources) for the archive's reporting period and licence.
