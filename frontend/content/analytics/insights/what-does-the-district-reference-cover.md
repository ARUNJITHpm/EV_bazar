## What can this reference tell us?

- A district code lets us join records without relying only on spelling.
- A renamed or split district needs a dated mapping before records from different periods can be compared.
- The source notes under the charts describe the archive and its limits.

Neither chart measures EV registrations, charger availability or charging energy. Those indicators need their own verified datasets. Read our [publication methods](/data/methodology) and [source register](/data/sources) before drawing conclusions about charging coverage.
