Scenario · fleet anchor

With a 200k kWh/yr take-or-pay anchor (3W aggregator or depot contract): NPV +₹9.8 L, IRR 21% — the anchor de-risks NPV and IRR. Breakeven is unchanged by design: it answers how busy retail must be.

Advice · sanctioned load

Full load needs 134 kVA. Managed-peak charging runs on 94 kVA and saves ₹2.7 L/yr in demand charges — recommended. Battery-buffered gets to 67 kVA if the DISCOM connection is the constraint.
